## 🌐 How to Deploy Your Website FREE

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Recommended — Free)
1. Create account at github.com
2. Create new repository named: `yourusername.github.io`
3. Upload index.html, style.css, script.js
4. Your site goes live at: `yourusername.github.io`

### Option 2: Netlify (Easiest — Free)
1. Go to netlify.com
2. Drag and drop the `website/` folder
3. Get a free URL instantly (e.g., taxease-pk.netlify.app)
4. Optionally connect your custom domain

### Option 3: Hostinger (Cheapest paid — PKR 500/month)
- Get yourname.com.pk domain + hosting
- Upload files via File Manager
- Most professional option

## 🔑 Critical Success Factors

### 1. Speed is Everything
- Reply to WhatsApp within 15 minutes during business hours
- Set up auto-reply for off-hours
- File returns within promised timeframe (builds trust)

### 2. Simplify the Process for Clients
- They don't want to learn about tax — they want it DONE
- Never use jargon in client communication
- Give them a simple checklist — not a tax lecture

### 3. Collect Testimonials Aggressively
- Ask every happy client for a review
- Screenshot positive WhatsApp messages
- Post testimonials every week on social media

### 4. Build for Retention
- Every client you file for comes back next year
- With 50 clients in Year 1, Year 2 starts with 50 guaranteed clients
- Add value throughout the year to keep clients loyal

### 5. Deadline Urgency is Your Marketing Superpower
- FBR deadline (Sep 30) creates natural urgency
- Ramp up marketing in August-September
- Use countdown timers and deadline warnings

## 🆙 Scaling Plan (Month 6+)

### When you hit PKR 300,000/month:
1. **Hire a part-time data entry person** (PKR 20,000/month)
   - They handle: document collection, data entry on IRIS
   - You handle: review, quality control, client relationships
   - Result: Double your capacity

2. **Start YouTube Channel**
   - 2-3 minute videos about common tax questions
   - Free, passive lead generation forever
   - Topic ideas included in social media templates

3. **Corporate Client Strategy**
   - Approach HR departments of medium-sized companies
   - Offer bulk employee return filing (PKR 2,000-2,500/employee)
   - 50 employees × PKR 2,000 = PKR 100,000 from ONE client!

4. **Partner with Accountants/CAs**
   - Many CAs don't do individual returns
   - They can refer clients to you for a referral fee
   - You can refer corporate work back to them
