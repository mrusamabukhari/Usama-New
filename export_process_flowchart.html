Step 01
ORDER CONFIRMATION
AKH sends Proforma Invoice with CIF Mogadishu price, product specs, HS codes, and payment terms. Buyer reviews and confirms order.
Proforma Invoice Issued
↓
💳
Step 02
ADVANCE PAYMENT — 50%
Buyer sends 50% advance via Dahabshiil (bank transfer), Western Union, or bank TT. AKH confirms receipt and begins production order.
Payment Confirmed ✅
↓
🏭
Step 03
FACTORY PRODUCTION
Faisalabad mill begins weaving Macawiis checked cotton / stitching Bed Linen / looming Towels to exact specifications. 100% cotton, correct GSM and size.
21–28 Days Production
↓
🔍
Step 04
QUALITY INSPECTION
AKH inspects every batch: fabric weight (GSM), dimensions, stitch count, colour matching, and labeling. Defective pieces rejected before packing.
AKH Quality Pass Required
↓
🏅
Step 05
BUREAU VERITAS — COC
Bureau Veritas inspector visits Karachi warehouse. Verifies quantity, quality, and labeling. Issues Certificate of Conformity (COC) — mandatory for Mogadishu customs clearance.
COC Certificate Issued
↓
📄
Step 06
DOCUMENT PREPARATION
Commercial Invoice • Packing List • Certificate of Origin (Chamber of Commerce) • Insurance Certificate • WeBOC/PSW Export Declaration • Form E (bank).
All Documents Ready
↓
⚓
Step 07
KARACHI PORT LOADING
Goods loaded into 20ft container at Karachi Port. Container sealed. Bill of Lading issued by Maersk / MSC / CMA CGM. Container number shared with buyer.
Bill of Lading Issued
↓
🚢
Step 08
SEA FREIGHT TO MOGADISHU
Maersk Musafir Express departs Karachi. Transit via Salalah (Oman) hub. Arrives Mogadishu Port. Live tracking available on Maersk website.
10–14 Days Transit
↓
🛃
Step 09
MOGADISHU CUSTOMS CLEARANCE
Buyer's clearing agent presents COC + Commercial Invoice + B/L to Somalia Customs. 10% import duty paid on CIF value. Container released in 3–5 days.
10% Duty on CIF Value
↓
🚛
Step 10
DELIVERY TO BUYER
Container transported to buyer's warehouse in Mogadishu. Goods unloaded and counted. Buyer confirms receipt matches invoice quantity and quality.
Goods Received ✅
↓
💰
Step 11
FINAL PAYMENT — 50% BALANCE
Buyer sends remaining 50% via Dahabshiil / Western Union / bank TT upon receiving goods. AKH confirms payment. Transaction complete.
Order 100% Complete ✅
