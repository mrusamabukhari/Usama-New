# Rice Export Plan: Pakistan → Somalia & Kenya

## Overview

This document outlines the plan for exporting rice from Pakistan to Somalia and Kenya.

## Export Origin

- **Country:** Pakistan
- **Key Export Regions:** Punjab (Basmati belt), Sindh (IRRI/non-Basmati rice)
- **Major Ports:** Port Qasim (Karachi), Karachi Port

## Destination Markets

### 1. Somalia
- **Capital:** Mogadishu
- **Main Entry Port:** Port of Mogadishu, Berbera (Somaliland)
- **Rice Demand:** High — rice is a staple food; Somalia imports the majority of its rice
- **Preferred Varieties:** Long-grain white rice (IRRI-6, IRRI-9), broken rice for affordability
- **Currency:** Somali Shilling (SOS) / US Dollar widely used
- **Key Buyers:** Wholesale traders, humanitarian organizations (WFP, NGOs)

### 2. Kenya
- **Capital:** Nairobi
- **Main Entry Ports:** Port of Mombasa (sea), Nairobi ICD (inland)
- **Rice Demand:** Moderate-high; growing urban middle class drives demand
- **Preferred Varieties:** Basmati (premium segment), long-grain parboiled (mass market)
- **Currency:** Kenyan Shilling (KES)
- **Key Buyers:** Supermarket chains, wholesalers, food processors

## Logistics & Shipping

### Route
- **Origin:** Port Qasim or Karachi Port, Pakistan
- **Destination (Somalia):** Port of Mogadishu (~3,500 km sea route)
- **Destination (Kenya):** Port of Mombasa (~4,200 km sea route)
- **Estimated Transit Time:** 12–18 days (direct vessel)
- **Shipping Method:** Bulk cargo or 20/40-ft containers (bagged rice)

### Packaging
- 25 kg and 50 kg PP woven bags (standard for both markets)
- Branding in English and Somali/Swahili as required by buyers

## Regulatory & Documentation Requirements

### Pakistani Export Side
- Export License from Trading Corporation of Pakistan (TCP) or private exporter registration
- Phytosanitary Certificate (issued by Pakistan's Department of Plant Protection)
- Certificate of Origin (issued by TDAP or Chamber of Commerce)
- Quality Inspection Certificate (REAP — Rice Exporters Association of Pakistan)
- Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading

### Somalia Import Side
- No formal import duty on food staples in most regions
- Halal certification recommended
- Compliance with Somali Bureau of Standards (SNBS) where applicable

### Kenya Import Side
- Import Declaration Form (IDF) via Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)
- Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) conformity assessment
- Phytosanitary import permit (Kenya Plant Health Inspectorate Service — KEPHIS)
- Halal certification (optional but preferred for Muslim consumer segments)
- Import duty: ~35% CET (Common External Tariff) under EAC; check for exemptions

| Item                        | Estimate (USD/MT)     |
|-----------------------------|-----------------------|
| IRRI-6 FOB Karachi          | $380 – $420           |
| Super Basmati FOB Karachi   | $900 – $1,100         |

- **Payment Terms:** Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight or 30-day usance; TT (wire transfer) for trusted buyers
- **Currency:** USD preferred for both markets

## Risks & Mitigation

| Risk                          | Mitigation                                      |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Somalia port instability      | Use Berbera port (Somaliland) as alternative    |
| Kenya high import tariffs     | Explore EAC duty exemption or bonded warehouse |
| Payment default               | Require L/C or advance payment for new buyers   |
| Quality rejection at customs  | Ensure REAP-certified quality pre-shipment      |
| Currency fluctuation          | Invoice in USD; hedge where possible            |
