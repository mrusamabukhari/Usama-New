## WHAT IS LEAD GENERATION?

```
Business owners have ONE biggest problem:
"I need more customers"

Lead Generation = You find potential customers FOR them
They pay you $500 - $5,000/month just to do this.

This is the HIGHEST VALUE service you can offer
because you directly make them money.
```

---

## HOW IT WORKS (Simple Version)

```
Example: Real Estate Agent in Lahore

Problem: Agent needs people who want to BUY or SELL property

Your Solution:
1. Create a simple Facebook ad:
   "Are you looking to buy property in Lahore? 
    Get free consultation!" 
   [Click Here → Form]

2. Interested people fill the form with their:
   • Name
   • Phone
   • Budget
   • Property type they want

3. You send these "leads" (contact info) to the agent

4. Agent calls them and closes deals

5. Agent pays you $500-2000/month because you
   helped him get 20-30 potential buyers per month!

YOUR COST: ~$100 in Facebook ads
WHAT CLIENT PAYS YOU: $1,500/month
YOUR PROFIT: $1,400/month per client ✅
```

## YOUR EXACT PROCESS

### Phase 1: Learn the Basics (Week 1-2, FREE)
```
Watch these FREE YouTube videos:
• "Facebook Lead Generation Ads Tutorial 2024"
• "How to run Facebook ads for beginners"
• "Google My Business for local businesses"

Time needed: 4-6 hours of watching
Cost: $0
```

### Phase 2: Get Your First Client FREE (Week 2-3)
```
Approach: Find a local business owner you know personally
          (uncle, neighbor, family friend, anyone)

Offer: "Let me run your Facebook ads for FREE for 
        1 month. I keep the ad spend low ($20-30).
        If I get you customers, you pay me PKR 15,000/month
        from month 2 onward."

This gets you:
• Real experience
• Testimonial
• First paying client
```

### Phase 3: Run Ads (Ongoing — 30 min/day)
```
Your daily task:
1. Check Facebook Ads Manager (10 min)
2. Check which ads are working (5 min)
3. Turn off bad ads, increase budget on good ones (10 min)
4. Reply to any client questions (5 min)
Total: 30 minutes per client per day
```
