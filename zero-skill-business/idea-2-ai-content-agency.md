## YOUR EXACT PROCESS (30 MINUTES PER CLIENT)

### Step 1: Client fills a simple form (5 min)
```
Questions you ask clients:
1. What is your business about?
2. Who is your target customer?
3. What topics do you want covered this month?
4. What tone? (Professional / Friendly / Funny)
5. Any keywords to include?
```

### Step 2: You give ChatGPT a prompt (5 min)
```
YOUR CHATGPT PROMPT TEMPLATE:

"You are a professional content writer for [business type].
Write a [blog post/caption/email] about [topic].
Target audience: [description]
Tone: [professional/casual/friendly]
Length: [word count]
Include these keywords: [keywords]
Make it engaging, helpful, and end with a clear call-to-action.
Business name: [name]
Business location: [city, Pakistan]"
```

### Step 3: Edit & personalize (10 min)
```
• Read through AI output
• Change 1-2 things to make it more specific
• Add client's business name and local references
• Done! Takes 10 minutes MAX
```

### Step 4: Send to client (2 min)
```
• Copy into Google Doc
• Share link with client
• "Here are your 4 blog posts for this month!"
• Client approves → Payment received ✅
```
