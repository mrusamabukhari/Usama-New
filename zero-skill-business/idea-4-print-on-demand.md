# 4️⃣ IDEA #4: Print-on-Demand Store
## No Inventory | No Shipping | Pure Passive Income

---

## HOW PRINT-ON-DEMAND WORKS

```
YOU                    PRINTFUL/PRINTIFY          CUSTOMER
Design a              When order comes in,        Buys your
T-shirt →  ────────→  they PRINT + SHIP  ───────→ product
                       automatically               online

You never touch the product.
You never handle shipping.
You never hold inventory.

You just create designs and collect money.
```

---

## THE BUSINESS MODEL

```
You list a T-shirt on your Etsy/Amazon store for: $25

```

## HOW TO CREATE DESIGNS (WITH ZERO ART SKILL)

### Tool 1: Canva (FREE)
```
• Go to canva.com
• Search templates: "T-shirt design"
• Pick a template, change the text
• Download as PNG
• Upload to Printful/Printify
Done in 15 minutes!
```

### Tool 2: Adobe Firefly AI (FREE)
```
• Go to firefly.adobe.com
• Type: "minimal mountain sunset T-shirt design, vector, white background"
• Download the AI-generated design
• Upload to your store
Done in 5 minutes!
```

### Tool 3: MidJourney / DALL-E
```
• Create AI art with a text prompt
• Use it as your T-shirt design
• No artistic skill needed whatsoever
```

## WHERE TO SELL

```
Platform 1: ETSY (BEST for beginners)
• Global audience, 90M+ buyers
• Cost: $0.20/listing + 6.5% fee on sale
• Best for: Unique, artistic, quirky designs

Platform 2: Amazon Merch on Demand (FREE)
• Biggest marketplace in the world
• FREE to join (apply and wait for approval)
• Best for: Simple text-based designs, popular niches

Platform 3: Daraz.pk (For Pakistan market)
• Local Pakistani market
• Partner with local printer (PrintPakistan, etc.)
• Accept JazzCash/EasyPaisa payments

Platform 4: Your own Shopify store
• Full control, no marketplace fees
• Requires marketing investment
• Best when you already have proven designs
```
