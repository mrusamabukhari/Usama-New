Your Journey

Month-by-Month Roadmap

Following the Social Media Agency model (recommended for beginners)

1-4
WEEKS 1-4 — Foundation
Setup & Get First Free Client
  • Create WhatsApp Business + Gmail for your agency
  • Learn Canva (1 hour on YouTube) + ChatGPT for captions
  • Create 5 sample posts (fake portfolio)
  • Walk into 10 local restaurants/salons — offer 1 FREE month
  • Land 1-2 free trial clients for testimonials
Income: $0 — Gaining Experience
M2
MONTH 2 — First Real Money
Convert Trials + Get Paying Clients
  • Convert 1-2 free trials to paying ($200-400/month)
  • Get 2 testimonials, post on social media
  • Send 15 outreach messages daily on WhatsApp/Facebook
  • Target: 3-4 paying clients total
Income: $600 - $1,200/month
M3
MONTH 3 — Building Systems
Raise Prices + Referral Machine
  • Raise pricing to $400-600/month (you have proof now)
  • Launch a referral program ($50 per referred client)
  • Create a simple website (Carrd.co — $19/year)
  • Target: 8-10 clients
Income: $2,000 - $4,000/month
M6
MONTHS 4-6 — First Hire
Hire a VA + Focus on Sales Only
  • Hire 1 virtual assistant on Fiverr ($150-200/month)
  • VA handles: content creation, scheduling, replies
  • You focus ONLY on: finding new clients + keeping current ones happy
  • Target: 20-25 clients
Income: $8,000 - $12,000/month
M12
MONTHS 7-12 — Scaling
Build Your Team + Premium Clients
  • Hire 2nd VA or part-time social media manager
  • Add higher-value services (paid ads, strategy — outsource these)
  • Land 2-3 corporate/company clients ($1,500-3,000/month each)
  • Target: 40-50 clients
Income: $18,000 - $25,000/month
Y2
YEAR 2 — Agency
Full Agency Mode
  • Team of 3-5 people (all hired cheaply via Fiverr/LinkedIn)
  • You work 2 hours/day on strategy and high-level decisions
  • Automate client onboarding, reporting, invoicing
  • Target: 80-100+ clients
Income: $35,000 - $50,000+/month ✅

Your Perfect 2-Hour Day

What to do every single day to build your $50K business

⏰ 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM

Weekend (30 min)

  • 📋Plan all content for next week in advance
