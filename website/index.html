FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who needs to file an income tax return in Pakistan?

Anyone with annual income above PKR 600,000, property owners, vehicle owners, and anyone who wants to benefit from lower withholding tax rates.

What is the FBR filing deadline?

The annual deadline is September 30th for individuals and December 31st for companies. Late filing can result in penalties.

Do I need to visit your office?

No! Our entire process is 100% online via WhatsApp and email. You never need to leave your home.

How do I make payment?

We accept JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and direct bank transfer. Payment is made after your return is ready for review.

What if I get an FBR notice after filing?

Premium clients get FBR notice response included. Basic and Standard clients can purchase this service separately.

Can you file for previous years too?

Yes! We can file returns for up to 5 previous tax years. Contact us for pricing on back-year filings.
